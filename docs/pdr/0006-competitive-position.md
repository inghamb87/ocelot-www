# PDR 0006: Compete with direct and Booking.com, not on price

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Source: Onyx listing page spec v7, sections A and E

## Context

A traveler considering an Onyx stay has four alternatives.

| Alternative | Its strength | How Onyx answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Book direct | Trusted | Same price. A person secures the room with no charge until confirmed. Adds the whole-trip concierge and trip credit. Direct often needs a deposit, offers no trip planning and shows thinner content. |
| Booking.com or Expedia | Fast. Sometimes slightly cheaper for a logged-in member. | Onyx does not fight on price. It wins on content, human confirmation, the concierge and the bundle. |
| Abandon and keep researching | The silent competitor for a careful buyer | Answer every question on the page: the honest read, rooms and villas, logistics, the wider trip. |
| Curated agencies (for example Mr & Mrs Smith) | Curation | They list some of the same hotels but move little volume. |

## Decision

- The page argues against booking direct, Booking.com and abandonment.
- Curated agencies are not treated as the primary alternative. Mention only in passing, if at all.
- The target guest is a couple, honeymooner, design-conscious or wellness traveler with higher willingness to pay and low price sensitivity. The page does not chase the discount-optimizing shopper.

## Consequences

- No price comparison tables and no "cheaper than" language anywhere.
- Listing pages carry more specific, useful content than the property's own site: logistics, a sample week, who it suits.
- Public copy does not name or disparage competitors beyond the neutral phrase "booking direct".
