# PDR 0009: The room is the primary conversion goal

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-24
- Source: Onyx listing page spec v7, sections 0 and F

## Context

The room carries most of the margin. Experiences add a secondary lift. A listing page could be pulled toward selling activities, newsletter signups or content engagement.

## Decision

- The primary conversion goal of a listing page is a submitted request to book.
- **Primary metric:** request to book submission rate.
- **Secondary metrics:** request-to-confirmation rate, confirmed booking rate, villa attach rate and resulting order value, film play and completion, ancillary attach rate and margin per booking, trip credit redemption, concierge engagement.
- Villas and other high-value options are made prominent because they raise order value and ancillary attach (groups, longer stays, private chef).
- Experiences are presented as what the concierge can arrange, not as a shop.
- Do not run cold paid traffic until the gating inputs in the [launch checklist](../launch-checklist.md) are resolved.

## Consequences

- Every listing section ends by serving the request: room cards link to the module with that room preselected, and a slim request bar follows the scroll.
- No analytics are installed yet. Choosing a privacy-respecting tool, and updating the CSP and privacy policy for it, is a launch task.
- The test backlog is in [experiments](../experiments.md).
