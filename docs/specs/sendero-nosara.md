# Property brief: Sendero Hotel & Villas, Nosara

Source: Onyx Property Listing Page Strategist (v7) output, supplied 2026-11-24. Public information only. Every flagged assumption is unverified until Onyx confirms it.

Changes from the earlier v5 run: rate access is now treated as clearable, and the Mr & Mrs Smith "incumbent" concern was removed because curated agencies drive negligible volume. Both raised this property's score.

## Confirmed (public sources, paraphrased)

- Design-forward boutique hotel and villas in Nosara. Opened 2023. Founded by two friends who met on a Nosara surf trip. Newer and more design-led than most of the town.
- 25 rooms and suites plus 7 private villas. Several villas have private plunge pools, kitchenettes and multiple bedrooms.
- Positioning is "luxurious simplicity": earthy, minimalist design, hacienda-style structures, repurposed brick and Costa Rican teak, a mature tree through the dining area, a gallery of local art (named artists, pieces for sale in rooms).
- Set beside a nature preserve of about 11 acres. A short jungle path (the "sendero") reaches Playa Guiones in roughly 4 to 5 minutes. Howler monkeys, coatis and iguanas on the property.
- Advertises itself as adult-oriented. Most popular with couples and solo travelers. Families fit best in the villas.
- Amenities: Sendero Kitchen (casual plus fine dining, frequently called the best kitchen in town), coffee truck, open-air bar, daily yoga and breathwork studio, in-house surf school and shop with rentals and photography, saltwater pool, free bikes, and access to Outpost (Nosara's private office membership club).
- Review signal is high and design-led: Tripadvisor 4.8 with a Travelers' Choice award, Booking.com around 9.1 with location near 9.5, strong repeat-guest loyalty.
- Caveats from public reviews: the pool is stylish but small and not built for a family pool day. The walk to town amenities and outside gyms is around 15 minutes on dusty unpaved roads. Queen beds in smaller rooms read closer to doubles.
- Public rates roughly US$261 to US$400 or more depending on room and date. Listed as 5-star. Genius discount eligible on Booking.com.
- Airport transfers can be arranged (around US$180 to US$200 for up to four). Roughly 2.5 hours from Liberia (LIR), about 10 minutes from the Nosara airstrip.
